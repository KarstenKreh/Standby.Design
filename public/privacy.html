Privacy Policy

standby.design & the standby-design MCP server · Last updated: June 12, 2026

The short version

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no trackers, and no analytics.

How the tools work

  • Web tools (standby.design/color, /type, /space, /shape, /symbol, /system): all computation happens in your browser. Your configuration lives entirely in the URL hash — nothing is sent to or stored on our servers.
  • MCP server (mcp.standby.design and the standby-design-mcp npm package): every request is computed statelessly and the response returned immediately. Inputs are design parameters (colors, ratios, sizes) — never personal data. Requests are not logged, stored, or shared. No authentication is required or offered.

Data retention

None. We have nothing to retain.

Third parties

The Type tool can load font previews from Fontshare (a free font service by the Indian Type Foundry) when you browse fonts in your browser. That request goes from your browser to Fontshare and is governed by their privacy policy. The MCP server makes no third-party requests at all.

Hosting

The site and MCP endpoint are hosted on a server in Germany (Hetzner, Nuremberg). Standard transport encryption (HTTPS) applies. Web server logs are limited to what the infrastructure requires for operation and are not used for tracking.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/KarstenKreh/Standby.Design.
